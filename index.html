ASTROMANCER'S SPIRE

An Interactive Low-Poly 3D Architectural Odyssey

THE WORLD

Perched high above the cloud line, the Astromancer's Spire rests at the confluence of celestial leylines. From the crooked slate tower that bends toward northern constellations, to the glowing pumpkin grove of alchemical draughts and the apprentice's cottage keeping watch over the horizons, each island floats in peaceful harmony.

INTERACTION

  • Scroll / Swipe: Journey down through the observatory, tudor study, ancient stone masonry, and glowing cauldron.
  • Atmospheres: Switch between Sunset, Night, and Day in the top-right dock to watch the sun and moon rise.
  • Explore: Switch to Free Orbit to inspect the architecture from any 3D angle.

CRAFT

Built with real-time WebGL & Three.js, procedural low-poly geometry, custom lighting shaders, and synthetic Web Audio.
